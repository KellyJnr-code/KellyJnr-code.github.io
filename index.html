What I've built

Project Auria
Solar-powered, GPS-equipped backpack for the RIT NSBE Imagine RIT exhibit
Hardware · Embedded
Built with the RIT NSBE Imagine RIT Project Committee, Auria is a solar-powered backpack designed to combat power access issues in areas with limited electricity, with an onboard GPS tracker for navigation. Sustainability drove every design choice, from solar charging to biodegradable backpack materials. I worked on the microelectronics team, wiring the solar panels to the battery through a TP4056 charging module and adding a boost converter to feed a wireless charger and USB port, while an ESP32 read live sensor data off the solar input to monitor the system. I later presented Auria as an NSBE exhibitor at Imagine RIT, walking attendees through the build.

Automated Greenhouse System
Closed-loop environmental control built on Arduino
Automation · Sensors
A class project built around an Arduino board that monitors temperature, light, motion, and water level, and reacts to what it senses instead of just logging it. When temperature drifts outside range, a fan kicks on; motion readings trigger an LED; and light sensor data adjusts an internal lamp, turning it on, dimming it, or shutting it off to hold consistent growing conditions. Water level is tracked as an alert reading to flag when attention is needed.
